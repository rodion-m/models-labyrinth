# Reasoning, knowledge, mathematics, and instruction-following benchmark map

## Scope and reading notes

This inventory was generated from the current `models_db.json` snapshot by selecting every unique `benchmark_id` beginning with `reasoning.`, `math.`, `knowledge.`, or `instructionFollowing.`. It contains **84 unique IDs** across **2,047 matching observations**: 4 instruction-following IDs / 179 observations, 39 knowledge IDs / 1,276 observations, 25 math IDs / 218 observations, and 16 reasoning IDs / 374 observations.

Dates below are upstream publication, competition, dataset, or documented-update dates. The snapshot's `fetched_at` values and `generated_at` are provenance for this local data pull, **not benchmark release dates**. `N/D` means that the cited primary source does not disclose the requested detail; it is intentionally not inferred. “AA” means Artificial Analysis. BenchLM is the local snapshot's republishing source, not treated as a canonical upstream source.

Many rows are deliberately aliases: the database preserves the vendor's displayed metric, implementation, tool condition, or aggregate as a distinct snapshot ID. They remain separate rows here, while the canonical upstream relationship is stated explicitly.

## Current-first workload priorities

For current evaluation work, prioritize the following in roughly this order:

1. **Current broad frontier and agentic signal:** Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.1.1 components (especially CritPt, AA-LCR, AA-Omniscience, HLE, GPQA Diamond, Terminal-Bench 2.1, SciCode, GDPval-AA v2, and τ³-Banking), plus FrontierMath V2 Tiers 1–3/Tier 4. These are the most useful current cross-model diagnostics in this snapshot, but AA is a composite vendor evaluation and should not be mistaken for a single benchmark.
2. **Long-context and interactive reasoning:** ARC-AGI-3, MRCR v2 and its 1M/128K–256K/64K–128K snapshot labels, LongBench v2, CorpusQA 1M, GraphWalks BFS 128K, and AA-LCR. Report context length, tool access, cache/scaffold, repeats, and effort because those conditions materially change the task.
3. **Current science, health, and research workflows:** CritPt, FrontierScience (Olympiad and Research), BioMysteryBench, GeneBench-Pro, HealthBench Hard/Professional/length-adjusted, SpatialBench Verified, MedXpertQA, ProteinGym-related rows, and the internal Anthropic life-science rows. Prefer these when the workload is scientific or clinical; several are private or internal and are not independently reproducible from public documentation.
4. **Current 2026 mathematics and contest anchors:** AIME 2026, HMMT February 2026, IMO 2026, USAMO 2026, June 2026 ArxivMath, RiemannBench, IPhO 2025 Theory, and the DeepSeek V4 APEX/Chinese-SimpleQA/CorpusQA rows. Contest answer accuracy is not equivalent to official proof grading.
5. **Older calibration anchors:** MMLU/MMLU-Pro/MMLU-Redux, GPQA, AGIEval, C-Eval, MMMLU, SimpleQA, GSM8K, MATH-500, BBH, DROP, IFEval, and LongBench-era measures. They remain useful for historical comparability, but saturation, leakage, stale task distributions, or known data-quality limits make them weak as the only current frontier signal.

## Primary-source interpretation notes

- **Artificial Analysis v4.1.1 is a current aggregate, not a benchmark release date for every component.** Its methodology says the current nine components are GDPval-AA v2, τ³-Banking, Terminal-Bench 2.1, SciCode, AA-LCR, AA-Omniscience, HLE, GPQA Diamond, and CritPt. The current article says HLE, AA-LCR, and AA-Omniscience graders were upgraded to GPT-5.6 Luna (medium), and τ³-Banking moved to upstream tau2-bench v1.0.1. IFBench was removed from the v4.1 aggregate for saturation, but its standalone ID remains in the snapshot.
- **FrontierMath V2 is not the same release as legacy FrontierMath.** Epoch reports a 2026-06-12 V2 correction/release with separate Tiers 1–3 and Tier 4 rows, private/held-out material, Python/SymPy answer functions, and a 1M-token limit with forced submission at 660K. The legacy row is retained solely for historical continuity.
- **Contest rows are source exams, not universal AI protocols.** AIME, HMMT, IMO, IPhO, and USAMO define human competition formats and grading; the snapshot's model score may be an answer-only vendor adaptation. Tool access, sampling, proof grading, and effort must therefore be treated as missing unless the specific run documents them.
- **Private/internal rows are intentionally conservative.** BioMysteryBench, CritPt, RiemannBench, FrontierScience, GeneBench-Pro, and several Anthropic life-sciences rows have official pages but do not publicly disclose every requested harness detail. Those cells say `N/D` instead of filling gaps from labels or secondary summaries.
